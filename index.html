Opportunity, Earned On And Off The Field.

Alumni In Action was established in 2019 as a non-profit organization. AIA promotes athletics at Francis Polytechnic High School in Los Angeles, as well as other Los Angeles inner-city schools — helping students aged 14 to 17 with a 2.0 GPA or greater.

What defines Alumni In Action isn't only who we help. It's how we show up — for the student, the parent, and the coach alike.

Through guidance and instruction, students in the inner-city gain a greater opportunity to succeed on the playing field, in the classroom, and in life.

AIA promotes athletics at Francis Polytechnic High School and other Los Angeles inner-city schools, creating opportunities for students, parents, and coaches in the community.

We conduct seminars for parents to get them more involved in their student's college preparation and future — because the family is part of the team.

Part of the plan for AIA is to promote fundraisers, provide scholarships, and receive donations from prominent businesses and organizations.

AIA also plans to distribute donated turkeys to families of under-privileged students in the Los Angeles area.

Four Ways We Back Our Students

[ 01/04 ]

On The Field

Athletic training and instruction that promotes athletics at Francis Polytechnic High School and other Los Angeles inner-city schools.

[ 02/04 ]

In The Classroom

A 2.0 GPA is the requirements for a student athlete to enter the door to University, and the standard we help students hold, so athletics and academics carry each other rather than compete.

[ 03/04 ]

With The Family

Seminars that bring parents into their student's college preparation and future, so nobody is navigating the next step alone.

[ 04/04 ]

Across The Community

Our annual charity golf tournament serves as the cornerstone for establishing student scholarships across Los Angeles.

“Never forget where you come from.”

Co-founder Craig Ellis grew up in South Central Los Angeles and passed through LA County Juvenile Hall as a teenager. Football took him to the Miami Dolphins and a record-setting CFL career. Now he coaches and mentors student athletes at Francis Polytechnic High School — and goes back to that same juvenile hall to speak to the kids inside.
