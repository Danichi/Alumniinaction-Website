[ 02 ] Where the money goes

Merchandise is a fundraiser, not a shop.

Part of the plan for AIA is to promote fundraisers, provide scholarships, and receive donations from prominent businesses and organizations. Merchandise is one of those fundraisers.

Craig Ellis's network of teams and star players from his time in the NFL and CFL supports our mission through donations, merchandise, and participation in our Charity Golf Tournament.
