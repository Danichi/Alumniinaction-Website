Charity Golf Tournament

Our flagship fundraiser, and the one our co-founder has run before. Craig Ellis founded the South Eastern Alberta CFL Alumni Golf Tournament in 1998 — it has since raised almost a million dollars and is still that United Way chapter's biggest fundraiser of the year.

Alumni In Action ran its own first annual tournament at Encino Golf Club, hosted by Polytechnic High School alumni. Craig's network across the NFL and CFL supports it through donations, merchandise, and participation.

Sponsorship and foursome enquiries are open now.
