Support our community, empower our youth

Your contribution to Alumni In Action makes a tangible difference in the lives of inner-city students in Los Angeles. By donating, you help us create opportunities for academic, athletic, and personal growth.

[ 01 ] Make an impact today

Where your donation goes

Scholarships

Your support helps fund scholarships for students in our program, opening doors that raw talent alone does not.

Parent seminars

It organizes vital seminars for parents on college preparation, so families can plan their student's future together.

Athletic training

It provides athletic training and instruction for student athletes at Francis Polytechnic and other Los Angeles inner-city schools.

Fundraisers

It enables us to promote fundraisers — including our Charity Golf Tournament — that keep the whole program running.

Holiday turkeys

It provides essential items like donated turkeys to underprivileged families during the holidays.

Guidance

Every donation, big or small, directly supports our mission to guide and train students aged 14 to 17 with a 2.0 GPA or greater.
