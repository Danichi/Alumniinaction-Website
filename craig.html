[ Co-founder ]

Craig Ellis

South Central Los Angeles to the NFL and the CFL — and back to the same juvenile hall where it started. This is the whole road, in his own words.

“Never forget where you come from.”

[ Words from my siblings ]

Growing up in South Central Los Angeles, every day was a mystery. Something could happen for the best or for the worst and trouble was never far away where I lived. Sports were an avenue to escape.

Alumni in Action is — and always will be — a way to support my childhood community by coming back to where it all began, Los Angeles County Juvenile Hall.

Craig Ellis, co-founder, Alumni In Action

[ 1970 ] The turn

Two people who did not give up on me

In 1970, I was arrested for breaking and entering, my first arrest, and sadly it wasn't the last. Most successful athletes give credit to their coaches or parents but my situation was different.

I owe it to an amazing woman by the name of Mary Ridgeway, my probation officer, and the well-groomed judge, Mr. Morrison. At the time, I never understood how important they were but through reflection of my life's trials and triumphs, it's now evident that these two pillars were an intricate part of my life.

[ The pros ] Miami, Los Angeles, Canada

Life rocketed me to the dream of my life

I learned a lot in college but football taught me the groundwork necessary for life's battles. The teamwork, structure, strategy, friendship, hard work, and prayers were my motivation.

Playing professional football gave me the chance to compete at the highest level and in the end, I was rewarded with teammates that became my brothers.

He carried the ball for the Miami Dolphins, has a name plate at the Raiders' plaza in Los Angeles, and went on to a decorated career in the CFL with Saskatchewan and Edmonton.

[ The record ] CFL

In the league's record book

Craig led the Roughriders in rushing and became the first running back in CFL history to lead the league in receiving. He was named Most Outstanding Player of the 1990 season.

He remains one of a short list of players in CFL history with more than one 100-catch season — Saskatchewan in 1985, Edmonton in 1990.

[ Today ] Coming full circle

Here to give back to those who got me here

Today, I'm here to give back to those who got me to where I am today. My fellow board members and I plan on being strong in the community by raising funds through donations and charitable events.

The common denominator of Alumni In Action? Each board member attended Francis H. Polytechnic, and together we're committed to making a difference.
